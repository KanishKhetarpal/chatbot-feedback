# Acharya admissions portal: how it works

Applicants apply at admissions.acharya.global. What is not stated here: say a counsellor will check it, never guess.

## Register and log in
- No password. Log in with your mobile number and a one-time code (OTP), sent on WhatsApp by default. International students can use email instead.
- The OTP is 6 digits and valid for 10 minutes. A new code can be requested after 60 seconds, at most 3 an hour. After 3 wrong tries the code locks: request a new one.
- Verifying the OTP creates the account; there is no separate sign-up. If the mobile or email is already registered, log in with it instead.
- A counsellor can send a personal login link that opens the portal without an OTP. Keep it private, like a password.
- You stay signed in while active; after about a week idle, log in again with an OTP.

## Steps
1. Basic details and course choice (campus, level, programme, specialisation, academic year).
2. Pay the application fee. The amount is shown in the portal for the programme and year. A coupon code must be entered before paying; some coupons work only in the Acharya mobile app.
3. Accept the terms. The full form then opens.
4. Personal details, then address.
5. Documents and academic details: upload the marks cards first and the marks fields fill in from them; check and correct them.
6. Declaration: tick consent, enter the place, optionally add a signature. This submits the application.
- Up to 3 applications per student, each programme/specialisation once. Details and documents carry over to your next application; the declaration is signed again for each.
- After submission the form and course cannot be edited: ask a counsellor.
- Application numbers look like AIT/2026/C00023.

## Documents
- Always: a profile photo and Aadhaar card. UG: Class 10 marks card (Class 12 optional). PG: Class 10 and Class 12 (degree optional). Diploma and PUC: Class 10. If your latest result is awaited, that document can come later.
- Also accepted: passport, transfer, migration, caste and income certificates, SOP, recommendation letter.
- PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each; photo and signature must be images.
- Upload once; it is used for all your applications. You can replace a file until it is reviewed; after that, only when the admissions team asks for a re-upload (you get a "Re-upload required" or "Document rejected" notice with the reason).

## Payment
- Online by Razorpay. A counsellor can also send a payment link that works without logging in; it may have an expiry date. If it has expired, ask for a new one.
- "Payment failed": no money is taken. If the portal does not let you pay again, or money left your account but the application still shows unpaid, contact admissions with the application number.
- After paying, the full form unlocks and a "Payment received" notice appears. Refund rules: see cancellation policy.

## Status meanings
- Application Initiated: fee not paid yet. Application Paid: form open. Application Submitted: under review.
- Offer Not Created: with the admissions team. Offer Sent: accept it in the portal within 7 days, or it expires. A conditional offer can be accepted only after its conditions are met.
- Offer Accepted, Payment Pending: pay the tuition fee. Paid, AUID Not Created: being processed. AUID Created: admission confirmed.

## Help
- Raise a query inside the portal (Admission, Documents, Fee Payment, Hostel, Transport, Academic, General; up to 2 MB attachment). Replies arrive as notifications.
